How these numbers work
Reporting basis: cash. Revenue counts when you record it as received; unpaid amounts are shown separately and not counted. Currency: USD. Dates use your business time zone ().
Gross profit = revenue − direct costs (materials, labor, and expenses tied to a service). Estimated operating profit = revenue − direct costs − your other recorded expenses. Operating margin = estimated operating profit ÷ revenue.
This is an estimate from what you've recorded. It isn't your personal income or cash you can take out.
