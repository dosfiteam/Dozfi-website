Questions worth asking.

Do I have to switch my booking system?

No. Dozfi sits alongside whatever you already use. You bring in your client and appointment data and Dozfi works from that. Nothing about your calendar, your till or your website has to change.

How long does setup take?

Most people are done in under ten minutes. You enter your business details, bring in your clients by pasting or uploading a spreadsheet, choose when reminders go out, and that's it.

What happens when a client replies NO?

Their appointment is released and you get a text straight away telling you who cancelled and what that slot was worth, so you can try to refill it while there is still time.

Can clients stop the texts?

Yes. Any client can reply STOP at any time and Dozfi stops messaging them immediately. That's required by carriers and it's handled for you.

What if Dozfi doesn't have enough data about my business?

Then it tells you that instead of inventing a recommendation. Each opportunity in your dashboard either shows real numbers from your data or explains exactly what to add before it can say anything useful.

Do I need to be good with technology?

No. If you can use a spreadsheet you can use Dozfi. And if you get stuck, email team@dozfi.com and a person replies the same day.

How do I cancel?

Log in, open Billing, and press Cancel my subscription. No email, no phone call. Cancel during your free trial and you are never charged.
