Questions worth asking.

Do I have to switch my booking system?

No. Keep booking the way you do now. You add your customers and appointments to Dozfi, by hand or from a spreadsheet, and Dozfi handles the reminders.

How long does setup take?

Most people are done in under ten minutes: add your customers, add their appointments, and turn on reminders.

What happens when a customer replies NO?

The appointment is marked Cancelled and you get a text right away saying who cancelled and what the appointment was worth, so you can try to fill the slot.

Can customers stop the texts?

Yes. Any customer can reply STOP at any time and Dozfi stops texting them immediately. Carriers require this, and it's handled for you.

What if a customer replies with a question?

Dozfi marks it Needs review and shows it on your Home page, so you can answer it yourself. It never guesses.

Do I need to be good with technology?

No. If you can send a text, you can use Dozfi. If you get stuck, email team@dozfi.com and a person replies the same day.

How do I cancel?

Log in, open Settings → Billing, and press Cancel subscription. No email, no phone call. Cancel during your free trial and you're never charged.
