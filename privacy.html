Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Dozfi ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use our SMS appointment reminder service.

Information We Collect

We collect information that business owners provide when signing up for Dozfi, including business name, email address, and phone number. We also collect client information that business owners upload, including client names and mobile phone numbers, for the purpose of sending appointment reminders.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to send automated SMS appointment reminders, win-back messages, birthday messages, and promotional notifications to clients who have opted in to receive such communications from their service provider through Dozfi.

SMS Messaging

By providing a mobile phone number to a business that uses Dozfi, you consent to receive SMS messages from that business via Dozfi. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. For help, reply HELP.

No Sharing of Mobile Information

We do not sell, rent, share, or disclose mobile phone numbers or any personally identifiable information to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile information collected through Dozfi is used solely to deliver the SMS services described in this policy.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Data Retention

We retain client data only as long as necessary to provide our services. Business owners may request deletion of their data at any time by contacting us.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at team@dozfi.com.
